# Additional public animation source candidates (2026-11-17)

SuperCartoons and AnimeAV1 now have opt-in adapters and passing live probes.
The remaining entries are research only. Do not count
mirrors of WCO or HiAnime as independent providers. Public catalogue access is
not proof of a direct playable stream or of access from the VPS.

* SuperCartoons: https://www.supercartoons.net/ — English classic cartoons.
  Public seed https://www.supercartoons.net/cartoon/the-cat-concerto/ exposes a
  direct MP4 in both structured metadata and the player config, with no account
  prompt. A useful candidate for classic cartoon shorts; TMDB movie/short-title
  matching differs from normal TV season numbering. Ranged MP4 verification and the adapter fixture/live probe pass. Registered
  provider, VPS and device checks remain separate acceptance steps.
* AnimeAV1: https://animeav1.com/ — the public site advertises Spanish-subtitled
  anime and exposes series/episode listings without a premium prompt. Seeds:
  https://animeav1.com/media/one-piece and its public episode links. Treat audio
  language separately from subtitle language. The public HLS adapter, exact identity checks and live One Piece probe pass.
  Registered provider, VPS and device checks remain separate acceptance steps.
* Anime-Sama: the old anime-sama.fr domain does not currently resolve from
  this host. Its self-described domain-status page https://api2.animesama.fan/
  links animesama.fan, but that destination returned403. Domain provenance and
  actual current playback must be established before implementation; do not
  choose a random search-result mirror.

Animepahe.si and AllAnime.to could not be fetched in this investigation;
AnimeKai.to did not resolve. They are not being presented as live providers.
No CAPTCHA, paid-account, DRM or token restrictions were removed or bypassed.
